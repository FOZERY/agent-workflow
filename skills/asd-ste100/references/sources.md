## Why the dictionary matters


STE controls vocabulary as well as sentence structure. Its general dictionary contains approximately 900 approved words.
A familiar English word can have a restricted meaning or part of speech.
Technical nouns and technical verbs follow separate categories. They are not unrestricted exceptions for difficult words.

Short sentences alone do not establish conformance. A model's self-assessment is not sufficient evidence of conformance.
The default output of this skill is an STE-based project profile. A full review needs the official standard and applicable terminology evidence.
ASD and STEMG do not endorse or certify this skill.

## Application decisions

- Apply the style to the text requested by the user or assigned by project instructions.
- Installation does not prescribe a project workflow or select a style for other outputs.
- Preserve the project's format, source language and publication permissions.
- For non-English artifacts, transfer the clarity principles and state the adaptation when assurance is discussed.
- Keep mandatory requirement words and exact technical tokens. Report an exception instead of silently changing their force or identity.

These are writing-profile adaptations, not requirements claimed on behalf of ASD.
No evidence reviewed here establishes improved code quality from applying STE to coding agents.
